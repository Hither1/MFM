The engine drains the bar slowly: about one point per 4,000 steps of the way gpt-6 plays. The
whole deficit of a 45k-step life is 3-5 food points -- one animal (raw mutton +2, cooked +6).
Six gen-2 lives ate 0 times with `eat` in the atom list and "no natural healing below 18" in
`references/survival.md` and the `starving` example reflex (which only writes a note). Gen 3b
got the food (sheep, cooked) and never ate either -- it never needed to. So the fix is small and
it is in the tick layer, not in a checklist: a reflex that EATS when the bar is below 18 and
food is carried, and one line of knowledge that an animal in view is one kill of food.
