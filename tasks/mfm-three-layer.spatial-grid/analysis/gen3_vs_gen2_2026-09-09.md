# Gen 3 against gen 2 on the same seed: what the new contract changed (2026-09-09, 19:50)

Compared: `of3r_mfm` (gen 2, the carry-only record, 16/24 at 41.6k steps / 13 h) and `of3g_mfm`
(gen 3, running since 12:56:51, 9/24 at step 10,000 after 6 h 20 min). Same world seed, same
model (gpt-6-astra high), same seed lineage (of3g carries of3 + of3r; of3r carried of3), same
one-thread session mode (`SESSION_TURNS=0`, `resume=yes` every turn). Different: the contract
text (AGENTS.md), the codex-native skills catalog, the [DIGEST] block, the plan-grain RECALL,
the frame size (640x360 vs 320x180), the provider (OpenRouter via proxy vs the nyu plan).
n = 1 life per generation; of3r was the best of three gen-2 lives (of1r 13, of2r 14+).

Evidence: `output/<arm>/episode_001/codex_turns/*.events.jsonl` (commands, usage),
`memory/logs.txt` (plan entries, steps, deaths), `logs/<arm>_purple.log` (turn and milestone
times), `memory/AGENTS.md` of each arm (the contract as received; diff in this session's
scratchpad, 15 lines removed, 83 added), `mcu_worldmodel/prompts.py:1495`, `agent.py:4295-4336`.

## 2. Change by change

**a. "Your context does not persist between turns" (prompts.py:1495).** Written for the
`SESSION_TURNS=1` plan (P1.1); the run uses one thread with `resume=yes`, compacted by codex
32 times in 217 turns (every ~7 turns). The model is told it forgets every turn, so it
rewrites working.md + digest.md every turn (0.68/turn) and validates actions.json every turn
(0.96/turn) -- bookkeeping that the thread makes unnecessary six turns out of seven.
Verdict: a defect, not a design choice. Fix: the sentence depends on the session mode; in
one-thread mode: "your thread persists until a compaction, which can come at any turn: keep
working.md current, do not rewrite it when nothing changed".

**b. [DIGEST] fed every turn + Routine "when you write notes.md, update digest.md".** The model
turned digest.md into a per-turn log: 207 lines, 100.6 KB, newest step-notes prepended
("Step10331 fifthstep stone BREAK VERIFIED ..."); the harness shows the first 3,000 chars
(`DIGEST_CAP`), i.e. the model's own last six step-notes -- the cross-life facts from the
workshop turn (deaths, lava, "natural stone does not bank mine_cobblestone") sit at the tail,
unseen. of3r's digest stayed 2.1 KB. The 100 KB file is what the next life will inherit.
Verdict: the digest lost its meaning. Fix: (1) the harness enforces the shape -- 20 lines, one
line per fact, truncates with a note like WORKING_CAP does; (2) the contract says what a
digest line is (a fact that holds across lives, never a step note) and drops the "update
digest when you write notes" routine; (3) the workshop turn rewrites it, later turns append
at most one line and only after a milestone or a death.

**c. "Rules of play" (the wz1119 process rules).** "Stop a plan the moment terrain, inventory
or the GUI differs from what it assumed", "verify clearance before advancing; inspect
footing, tool and surroundings after a hold", "before crafting inspect ingredients, recipe
layout, quantities, cursor, destination slots". These are always-on and the model obeyed
them literally: on open ground it hopped one block at a time and looked down after each hop
(14:03-17:40, 6/24 -> 7/24 took 4,250 steps; of3r took 2,500). Gains: 0 deaths in 10k steps,
food, torches and a sword in hand before the first descent (the Routine), no GUI accident.
Costs: plan grain halved on the stages where nothing threatens. Verdict: right rules at the
wrong scope. Fix: scope them -- the crafting rule into `references/gui.md`/the GUI atoms,
"clearance/footing" into the `mining` skill and the descent Routine, keep in the contract
two lines (stop on surprise; budget before a descent) and add the pace rule the contract
lacks: "on known or open ground plan 60+ steps; verify at the end of the plan".

**d. Look Routines** ("view_image before you walk to a remembered place, after a hit, when a
plan surprised you"; "--then-now on arrival"). Looks went from 0.01 to 0.70 per turn. They did
find life 1's iron entrance (7/24 at 17:56, via `--then-now` against ep_000231 of life 1) --
the across-life frame channel works -- but at a look per turn on a 108-s turn it is the
single largest time item after the writes. Verdict: keep the channel, cap the habit: one look
per plan unless health dropped or the plan's outcome surprised; `--then-now` once on arrival,
not on every approach step. P3-C (text-only control) measures what the looks buy.

**e. Codex-native skills catalog.** Trigger descriptions are broad ("Use when a stage begins or
a game rule is in doubt"; memory: "before walking to a remembered place, when a plan has
failed twice") so a SKILL.md is read 0.73 times per turn, and after every compaction the
model re-reads memory/minecraft/harness. No model-authored skill in 227 turns (mine/skills
empty in both generations): the "Improve yourself" paragraph is a standing instruction with
no moment attached, and the model never reached the "worked twice" trigger under 6-step
plans. Verdict: the catalog costs more than it returns so far. Fix: narrower triggers (one
per stage: "the first descent", "the first Nether turn", "after YOU DIED"), a line in the
contract "a skill is read once per stage, not per turn", and a concrete moment for
self-improvement: at each milestone the harness's [NOTE] asks "what worked twice since the
last milestone? procedure or skill, or nothing".

**f. `episodes.py --ores/--y-profile`, queries 0.51 per turn.** Used heavily and to the point
(iron came from life 1's record). Cost is now small (index-backed, no longer 85-120 s).
Verdict: keep.

**g. Plan-grain RECALL.** The [RECALL] block is in 224/229 prompts and an entry-level "X
changed nothing in its last N calls" line in 169/229 -- in of3r 468/519, mostly `watch`,
which by design changes nothing. A signal present in 75-90% of turns is noise. Verdict: the
plan-grain line (P1.4b) is right; the entry-grain lines should drop `watch`, `look*`,
hotbar and any entry whose purpose is not to change the world.

**h. Own reflexes, no harness reflexes.** 4 reflexes copied in the workshop turn (fell, hit,
known_hazards, lava_ahead), 0 deaths so far; of3r's single death was in the Nether at 41.9k.
Verdict: neutral at this stage; the Nether decides.

**i. Frame 640x360.** +216 tokens per frame; the model reads hotbar counts it could not read
at 320x180, which the harness also gives as text. Whether richer frames feed the look habit
is not separable in this run. Verdict: unknown; P3-C/D isolate it.

**j. OpenRouter through the proxy.** Two stream reconnects in 6 h (16:44 "502", 17:31 "error
decoding response body"), both recovered on the first retry; no 403. Generation speed
against the nyu plan is not measured. Cost ~$19/h. Verdict: works; speed unknown.

**k. Contract size.** 15.8 KB against gen 2's 13.9 KB (the plan's target was <= 8 KB): the
procedures list was compressed (-2 KB) and Routines + Rules + Improve added 3.4 KB. In one
thread the size matters less than in fresh sessions, but every compaction re-reads it.

**l. Infrastructure, not the contract.** 13:12-13:16 the root filesystem filled (a shared-node
job), codex's sandbox could not start, 3 turns lost; the harness moved on. Not attributable
to gen 3.

## 3. What gen 3 got right

The workshop turn reads both lives, copies the two useful procedures (west_stairs,
west_level) and four reflexes, and writes a digest whose tail is exactly what a digest
should hold (the Nether piglin death, the lava lake, the cobblestone-banking rule). Memory
reuse produced the iron (life 1's entrance, confirmed by then-now). The descent Routine
produced a prepared descent (food, torches, blocks, sword; cooked mutton in the inventory).
Zero deaths in 10k steps. The catalog, the index queries and the frame channel all function.

## 4. Ranked changes for gen 3b (contract and harness only; seed, provider, frames unchanged)

1. Session-mode-aware persistence sentence (a). L0, prompts.py.
2. Digest discipline: harness cap with a note, contract wording, no per-turn digest writes (b).
   L0/L1, agent.py + prompts.py.
3. Scope the Rules of play into the stages and add the pace rule (c). L1, prompts.py + skills.
4. Look and skill budgets: one look per plan; a skill once per stage (d, e). L1.
5. A moment for self-improvement: the milestone [NOTE] asks for the procedure/skill (e). L1.
6. RECALL entry-grain noise: drop non-world-changing entries (g). L0, episodes.py.
7. Contract to <= 10 KB by moving the moved rules out (k).

Decisive experiment: of3g2 = the same launch (`launch_g3.sh 3`, OpenRouter, 640x360, seed
of3r) with 1-6 applied; read-outs at equal wall: steps per turn (target >= 100), turns to
step 10k (target <= 100), milestone times against of3r's, digest size (<= 2 KB), looks and
skill reads per turn (<= 0.2), deaths. If steps per turn recover and the milestone clock
tracks of3r's, the gen-3 memory features can be judged on their own; if not, P3-B
(from-scratch) and P3-C (text-only) separate the frame and the memory from the contract.
